## The Vision

I was tired of bookmark apps crammed with features I didn't want, asking for a subscription just to save a URL. I found a brilliant open-source app called Savr, took its solid foundation, and started building the exact tool I wanted to use. 

Shelf does exactly one thing, and it does it exceptionally well: **drop a link, grab the details, move on.** I've added several new features to the original source code, and I'll be actively bringing even more additions in the future.

No sign-ups. No cloud lock-in. No trackers. Just you and the links that matter.

## Why you'll love it

✨ **Magic Metadata.**  
Paste a URL, and watch Shelf instantly pull in the title, description, and preview image. It just works.

🗂️ **Collections. Perfected.**  
Group your bookmarks naturally. No messy nested folders—just name a collection, drop your links in, and find them effortlessly later.

⚡ **Peek before you open.**  
A single tap gives you a quick, beautiful preview. See what's inside without ever leaving the app.

🔍 **Search that actually works.**  
Find exactly what you're looking for, the moment you need it, by searching across titles and URLs instantly.

🎨 **Designed for your screen.**  
With full Material You support, Shelf doesn't just run on your phone; it belongs there. It adapts to your wallpaper seamlessly, looking gorgeous in Light, Dark, Oled or System mode.

🔄 **Your data. Your rules.**  
You own your bookmarks. Export them to JSON or HTML anytime. Import from your desktop browser just as easily. 

💾 **Peace of mind, built-in.**  
Rest easy with automatic, daily backups saved straight to your device. You never lose a thing.

## Under the Hood

Built with the best modern Android has to offer:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Language** | Kotlin 💜 |
| **UI** | Jetpack Compose + Material 3 |
| **Architecture** | MVI + StateFlow |

| **Networking** | OkHttp, Jsoup |
| **Images** | Coil |
